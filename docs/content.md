# 《Concepts Need Places》新版目录草案

本书保留四组核心框架：

1. Body / Mind（身体 / 心智）
2. Language / Work（语言 / 工作）
3. Relationship / Society（关系 / 社会）
4. Technology / Value（技术 / 价值）

八个 Theme 仍然分开写。每个 Theme 不再拆成“总览、Group、Container 示例”三章，而是改为一个 Theme Toolbox（主题工具箱）章节。章节主体是 Container，但 Container 不是知识罗列，而是帮助读者熟悉一个领域如何谈论世界、形成区分、跟随因果链。

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Foreword: Why Concepts Need a Place

1. Answers are easy; placement is scarce.（答案变容易，安置变稀缺。）
2. Language ability is concept placement ability.（语言能力是概念安置能力。）
3. A concept without a place cannot be called when needed.（没有位置的概念无法被稳定调用。）
4. This book builds a personal RU tree.（本书构建个人 RU 树。）
5. Read for use, not for coverage.（为使用而读，不为覆盖面而读。）

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Method and Map

## Chapter 1. Subject Gain Principle

核心问题：What deserves to enter the reader's core system?（什么值得进入读者的核心系统？）

本章说明主体增益原则：一个概念只有能改善 understanding, judgment, action, state, expression, creation, or long-term capability（理解、判断、行动、状态、表达、创造或长期能力），才值得进入主树。

## Chapter 2. Referable Unit: What Deserves a Place

核心问题：What can be treated as a unit in thought and language?（什么可以在思考和语言中被当作一个单位？）

本章定义 RU / Referable Unit（可指称单位），说明 RU 不是名词，而是可以被指称、分类、安置、连接和调用的语义单位。

## Chapter 3. Four Layers: Theme → Group → Container → Leaf

核心问题：How does a concept get a stable place?（一个概念如何获得稳定位置？）

本章说明四层结构，并强调正文重点是 Theme、Group、Container。Leaf 无限，主树有限。

## Chapter 4. The Eight-Theme Map

核心问题：How does the whole personal system hang together?（整个个人系统如何连在一起？）

本章给出八主题总图：

Body / Mind
Language / Work
Relationship / Society
Technology / Value

重点不是列出八个领域，而是说明它们如何形成个人运行系统：inner operation（内在运行）、expression and action（表达与行动）、connection and position（连接与位置）、extension and judgment（扩展与判断）。

# Part 2: Body / Mind

## Chapter 5. Body Toolbox

Opening problem: Why do I feel unable to think, even when I know what to do?（为什么我明明知道该做什么，却没有能力去想、去做？）

Domain language: Body talks about energy, sleep, stress, sensation, pain, recovery, and physical limits（身体领域谈论能量、睡眠、压力、感官、疼痛、恢复和身体限制）。

Core containers should be selected from Energy, Sleep, Diet, Movement, Health, Sensation, Stress, Fatigue & Pain.

The chapter must include common misplacements, at least one causal chain, and placement practice.

## Chapter 6. Mind Toolbox

Opening problem: Why do I keep making the same decision error?（为什么我反复犯同一种决策错误？）

Domain language: Mind talks about perception, attention, memory, emotion, motivation, cognition, learning, and self（心智领域谈论感知、注意、记忆、情绪、动机、认知、学习和自我）。

Core containers should be selected from Perception, Attention, Memory, Emotion, Desire & Motivation, Cognition, Learning, Self.

The chapter must include common misplacements, at least one causal chain, and placement practice.

# Part 3: Language / Work

## Chapter 7. Language Toolbox

Opening problem: Why can I understand something vaguely but fail to say it clearly?（为什么我模糊地懂了，却说不清楚？）

Domain language: Language talks about naming, wording, sentences, concepts, questions, arguments, narratives, and instructions（语言领域谈论命名、措辞、句子、概念、问题、论证、叙事和指令）。

Core containers should be selected from Word & Phrase, Sentence, Text, Concept, Question, Argument, Narrative, Instruction.

## Chapter 8. Work Toolbox

Opening problem: Why does effort fail to become skill gain?（为什么努力没有转化成能力收益？）

Domain language: Work talks about goals, plans, tasks, methods, projects, practice, problems, and feedback（工作领域谈论目标、计划、任务、方法、项目、练习、问题和反馈）。

Core containers should be selected from Goal, Plan, Task, Method, Project, Practice, Problem, Feedback.

# Part 4: Relationship / Society

## Chapter 9. Relationship Toolbox

Opening problem: Why do connection, boundary, trust, and conflict keep getting mixed together?（为什么连接、边界、信任和冲突总是混在一起？）

Domain language: Relationship talks about connection, trust, boundary, intimacy, cooperation, conflict, influence, and relational roles（关系领域谈论连接、信任、边界、亲密、合作、冲突、影响和关系角色）。

Core containers should be selected from Connection, Trust, Boundary, Intimacy, Cooperation, Conflict, Influence, Relational Role.

## Chapter 10. Society Toolbox

Opening problem: Why does personal effort sometimes fail because of position, rule, or market structure?（为什么个人努力有时会被位置、规则或市场结构限制？）

Domain language: Society talks about roles, groups, organizations, institutions, markets, rules, careers, and platform ecology（社会领域谈论角色、群体、组织、制度、市场、规则、职业和平台生态）。

Core containers should be selected from Role, Group, Organization, Institution, Market, Rule, Career, Platform Ecology.

# Part 5: Technology / Value

## Chapter 11. Technology Toolbox

Opening problem: Why does a tool sometimes extend ability, but sometimes create more complexity?（为什么工具有时扩展能力，有时却制造更多复杂性？）

Domain language: Technology talks about tools, software, AI models, data, systems, platforms, automation, and workflows（技术领域谈论工具、软件、AI 模型、数据、系统、平台、自动化和工作流）。

Core containers should be selected from Tool, Software, AI Model, Data, System, Technology Platform, Automation, Workflow.

## Chapter 12. Value Toolbox

Opening problem: Why do I know what is possible, but still not know what is worth doing?（为什么我知道什么可行，却不知道什么值得做？）

Domain language: Value talks about importance, benefit, cost, risk, purpose, standard, ethics, and meaning（价值领域谈论重要性、收益、成本、风险、目的、标准、伦理和意义）。

Core containers should be selected from Importance, Benefit, Cost, Risk, Purpose, Standard, Ethics, Meaning.

# Part 6: Use and Training

## Chapter 13. How to Place a New Concept

This chapter teaches the placement workflow:

1. Identify the RU.
2. Test subject gain.
3. Choose the primary position.
4. Add associated positions.
5. Write associated language.
6. Write use sentences.
7. Add it to the personal RU tree.

## Chapter 14. How to Diagnose a Problem with the RU Tree

This chapter teaches how to use the RU tree for diagnosis:

1. Is this a Body, Mind, Language, Work, Relationship, Society, Technology, or Value problem?
2. Is the current concept misplaced?
3. Which associated positions are being ignored?
4. What causal chain explains the current difficulty?

## Chapter 15. How to Maintain and Train Your System

This chapter combines daily training, weekly review, pressure testing, and pruning（修剪）. The goal is not to collect more concepts, but to make the existing system more callable（更可调用）.
